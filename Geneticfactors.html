Genetic Factors

Exploring gene correlations associated with Parkinson’s disease.

Understanding the Gene Graph

This graph shows genes that are correlated with Parkinson’s disease. Connections between circles represent relationships between genes, and the different color groups correspond to different pesticides. Something we want this visualization to make you consider is:

  • Do certain pesticides affect certain genes, causing them to mutate?
  • How closely are certain genes related to the disease?
