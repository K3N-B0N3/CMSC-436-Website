Understanding Parkinson’s Disease

Exploring genetic, environmental, and epidemiological factors

Project Overview

Parkinson’s disease is an incurable disease that affects mobility and brain function. This disease is well-known but poorly understood. There has been basic research done into the existence of Parkinson’s in the United States. It is this research that we have decided to investigate and to create visualizations for. Primarily, our goal is to show some of the relevant factors to Parkinson’s disease. There are many factors, both genetic and environmental, that research has shown to have connections to Parkinson’s. A user should be able to explore these factors and make a basic evaluation about which factor may have a greater contribution to the disease’s development.

About This Website

This website is the end result of our investigation into Parkinson’s research. The different tabs at the top of the page will allow you to explore the visualizations our group has created for this project.

How to Use This Website

  • Use the navigation bar at the top of the page to explore different factors related to Parkinson’s disease.
  • The Disease Prevalence/Incidence tab shows choropleth maps of Parkinson’s cases.
  • The Environmental Factors tab shows pesticide and pollution visualizations.
  • The Genetic Factors tab shows gene correlation networks.
  • The Correlation of Factors tab compares multiple variables together.
  • You can return to this Home page at any time by clicking the Home button.
